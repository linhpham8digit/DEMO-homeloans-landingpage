How I work

Four steps, in order. Nothing skipped.

A loan is a means, not the goal. We move through it the same way every time, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Income, equity, goals and constraints, set out plainly before any recommendation.

  2. 02

    Strategise

    The plan first: structure, offset, term and sequence, shaped for where you are headed.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    A clear comparison across lenders, with the reasoning written down. You decide. We lodge.

  4. 04

    Stay on it

    We review the position as rates, equity and plans move, for as long as you want a partner.
